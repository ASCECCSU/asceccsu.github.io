# Landing Page

**American Society of Civil Engineers | Central Connecticut State University Student Chapter**

</div>

---

## Specifications

#### 🌟 Dynamic Header

Animated showcase of key ASCE | CCSU information with smooth transitions.

#### 🎨 Theme Switcher

Seamlessly toggle between:

- CCSU Blue
- Dark Mode
- ASCE White

#### ✨ Join ASCE | CCSU

Prominent call-to-action directing to [Club Central](https://clubcentral.ccsu.edu/organization/americansocietyofcivilengineers).

#### 🔗 Social & Links

"Connect & Explore With Us":

- Social platforms icons
- Club Central footer icon
- Live Instagram feed integration
- ASCE | CCSU in-house LinkCrane
  - Preceded by Linktree

#### 🥚 Easter Egg

Hidden ASCII art in the site's code.
